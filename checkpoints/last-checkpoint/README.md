## Skills I Gained During the Bootcamp

### Low-Level Design (LLD)

I learned how to design the internal structure of an application, including classes, components, interfaces, and their interactions. This helped me better understand how to write clean, organized, and maintainable code.

### High-Level Design (HLD)

I developed a better understanding of how to plan an application's overall architecture, identify its main components, and consider scalability, performance, and reliability before implementation.

### Microservices Architecture

I learned the principles of breaking a large application into smaller, independent services. I also gained a better understanding of service communication, separation of responsibilities, and the challenges of distributed systems.

### MongoDB and Document Databases

I explored NoSQL concepts, especially MongoDB and document-oriented data storage. I learned how document databases differ from relational databases and why they can be useful when applications need flexible data models.

### Problem-Solving and Software Engineering Mindset

Beyond specific technologies, the bootcamp encouraged me to analyze requirements, compare design choices, and consider the long-term impact of technical decisions.
