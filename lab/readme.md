# Master's Project Presentation: Artparts.ci

**Project Title:** Artparts.ci – An Intelligent Real Estate Platform for Property Sales and Rentals with Virtual Tours and Lead Management.

### Project Overview

Artparts.ci is a modern web-based real estate platform designed to simplify the process of buying, selling, and renting properties in Côte d'Ivoire.

The project aims to address several challenges in the real estate sector, including unreliable property listings, time-consuming physical visits, and difficulties faced by real estate professionals in managing and following up with potential clients.

To overcome these challenges, Artparts.ci will provide a centralized digital platform connecting property seekers, property owners, and real estate agencies.

### Project Objectives

The main objective is to design and develop a user-friendly, secure, and intelligent real estate platform with the following features:

- **Property Management:** Allow property owners and agencies to publish, manage, and update property listings for sale or rent.
- **360° Virtual Tours:** Enable users to explore properties remotely through immersive virtual tours, reducing the need for unnecessary physical visits.
- **Interactive Maps:** Display property locations on a map to help users find properties in their preferred areas.
- **Lead Generation and Management:** Help real estate professionals capture, qualify, and track potential customers interested in their properties.
- **Smart Property Recommendations:** Suggest relevant properties based on users' preferences, budgets, and search criteria.
- **Appointment Scheduling:** Allow users to book physical property visits and communicate with property owners or agents.

### Technologies

The platform will be developed using modern web technologies:

- **Frontend:** Next.js with TypeScript.
- **Backend:** Node.js with Express.js and TypeScript.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL.
- **Virtual Tours:** 360° panoramic visualization technologies.
- **Mapping:** Interactive maps and geolocation services.

### Innovation and Added Value

The main innovation of Artparts.ci lies in combining three essential components: **real estate listings, immersive virtual property tours, and intelligent lead management.**

Unlike a traditional property listing website, Artparts.ci aims to provide a more interactive and efficient experience for property seekers while offering real estate professionals tools to improve customer engagement and sales opportunities.

The integration of smart recommendations and lead management features will also help agencies better understand customer needs and manage their prospects more effectively.

### Expected Outcome

By the end of the project, the expected result is a fully functional web application that allows users to search for properties, explore them through virtual tours, contact property owners or agencies, and schedule visits.

Real estate professionals will also be able to manage their listings, track potential customers, and monitor their commercial activities through a dedicated dashboard.

### Conclusion

Artparts.ci aims to contribute to the digital transformation of the real estate industry in Côte d'Ivoire by making property transactions more accessible, transparent, and efficient.

**The long-term vision is to build an innovative real estate platform that connects property seekers and real estate professionals through technology, virtual experiences, and intelligent customer management.**
